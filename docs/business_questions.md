# Business Questions

Every dashboard visual must answer at least one of these questions.

## A. Market Overview
1. How many sale and rent listings are in the market, and what is the typical (median) price and price per sqm?
2. How does the market split by property type and by finishing level?

## B. Location
3. Which cities and areas have the highest and lowest median price per sqm?
4. How far is each area from its city's median (premium or discount %)?
5. Which areas have the most supply (listing count)?

## C. Property Characteristics
6. How does price per sqm change with size band and number of bedrooms?
7. How much does finishing level (fully / semi / core & shell) change the price per sqm?
8. How strongly are area (sqm) and price related, and how much of the price variation do size, bedrooms, finishing and location explain?

## D. Investment & Valuation (project differentiator)
9. Which areas have the highest **gross rental yield**, and what is the payback period in years?
10. Which listings are **overpriced** or **opportunities** compared with their own area + property type?
11. Do ready-to-move properties differ in price per sqm from under-construction ones?

## E. Time (only if listing dates exist)
12. How does median price per sqm move month over month?

## F. Data Quality
13. How many listings were removed or flagged, and why? (shown in the cleaning log)

## Stakeholder Persona (to keep the dashboard focused)
A first-time investor or analyst in Egypt who wants to know **where to buy, what price is fair, and where renting pays best**.
